# **1. Terms and Conditions of Sale (TCS) – English Version**

## 1. Purpose

These Terms and Conditions of Sale govern all orders placed on the website Asiacuisine.re for meal delivery or pickup services as offered by the micro-enterprise Asiacuisine.re.

## 2. Products and Services

The weekly menu, meal formulas, and available pickup or delivery locations are published on the website’s “Menu of the Week” page and may also be communicated through social media. Product availability may vary depending on supply conditions and preparation capacity.

## 3. Orders

Orders must be placed at least **48 hours before the delivery day, before 11:00 a.m.**
An automated system on the website restricts the selection of delivery dates to those that comply with these rules.

Orders are confirmed only after:

* validation of the selected items,
* submission of required customer information, and
* receipt of a payment link or bank details (RIB), followed by full payment.

Asiacuisine.re reserves the right to refuse or cancel any order in the event of abnormal demand, incomplete information, or non-payment.

## 4. Prices

Prices are listed on the website and expressed in euros, inclusive of taxes when applicable. Asiacuisine.re reserves the right to adjust prices at any time; however, any order already confirmed will be billed at the price shown at the time of purchase.

## 5. Payment

Payment must be made in advance. No cash payments are accepted.
Payment methods include:

* online payment via secure link,
* bank transfer using the provided RIB.

Orders not paid by the specified deadline will not be prepared.

## 6. Delivery and Pickup

Delivery and pickup locations are announced on the website and social media.
Customers must be present at the agreed location and time.

Any order not collected within the agreed time may be considered forfeited.
To avoid waste, uncollected meals may be redistributed or donated to an association, depending on feasibility and agreements with partner organizations.

## 7. Modifications, Cancellation, and Refunds

Given the nature of fresh, made-to-order food preparation, orders are non-refundable after payment.
Modification requests may be considered only if submitted before the preparation process begins. Acceptance remains at the discretion of Asiacuisine.re.

## 8. Allergies and Special Diets

Information on ingredients is provided for transparency. However, despite all precautions, cross-contamination cannot be entirely ruled out.
Customers with allergies or specific dietary requirements must inform Asiacuisine.re before placing an order. The company cannot be held responsible for reactions resulting from uncommunicated allergies.

## 9. Liability

Asiacuisine.re is responsible for the proper execution of the order, within the limits of factors that are under its control.
The company cannot be held liable for delays caused by force majeure, transportation disruptions, or customer unavailability.

## 10. Personal Data

Personal data collected is used exclusively for order processing, payment management, and delivery or pickup coordination.
Data is handled in compliance with current GDPR rules.
Customers may request access, correction, or deletion of their personal data by contacting Asiacuisine.re.

## 11. Applicable Law

These Terms and Conditions are governed by French law.
Any dispute shall fall under the jurisdiction of the competent courts of La Réunion.

* 选择产品并确认数量；
